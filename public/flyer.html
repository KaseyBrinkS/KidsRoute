KidsRoute

Safe group walks for kids — to school & after-school activities

KidsRoute helps NYC families coordinate safe group walks for kids — to school, after-school activities, and everywhere in between. Match with neighboring families heading the same way, and get real-time check-ins so you always know your child arrived safely.

Because raising kids takes a village — and KidsRoute helps you find yours.

How it works

  1. 1

    Add your kids

    Create profiles with school and grade.

  2. 2

    Add their activities

    Enter where they go and when.

  3. 3

    Get matched

    We notify you when neighbors walk the same route.

  4. 4

    Walk together

    Join the group, meet up, and track check-ins.

Why families love KidsRoute

  • School walks — morning drop-off & afternoon pick-up with building neighbors
  • After-school activities — match families going to the same classes & programs
  • Walk home groups — coordinate rides home from activities
  • Real-time check-ins — know when your child is picked up and dropped off
  • Group chat & meeting points — plan walks with other parents
  • Privacy first — your info is only shared with matched families
  • Free for families — sign in with Google
